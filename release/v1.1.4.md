# The Minish Cap for PS Vita – v1.1.4

A bug-fix update for v1.1.3. Install over the old version with VitaShell: your saves and extracted files in `ux0:data/tmc/` are kept.

## Download
- `mc-vita-v1.1.4.vpk`: install with VitaShell.

## Fixed
- **Gleerok's lava flood (Cave of Flames boss).** When Gleerok dives into the lava after being hit, the lava rising over the room is visible again, as on the GBA. Before, it showed only as a faint shadow.
  - This came from a change Project Picori made to the boss to avoid a freeze on PC. The Vita build now plays the original sequence, with a safety net: if the boss hasn't resurfaced after 25 seconds, it resurfaces anyway.
